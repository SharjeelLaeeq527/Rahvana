Frequently Asked Questions

How often does USCIS update cases?

USCIS statuses update nightly during scheduled batch processing, typically between 11 PM and 6 AM Eastern Time.

Does checking hurt my case?

No, querying your case status is a read-only request to public records and has zero negative impact on adjudication.
