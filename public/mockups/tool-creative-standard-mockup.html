Help & Guidance

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers on official guidelines, legal requirements, and common issues for this tool.

How often does USCIS update case statuses? +

Statuses update nightly during scheduled batch processing, typically between 11 PM and 6 AM Eastern Time.

Does querying my status impact my case? +

No, this query is a read-only request to official public USCIS records with zero impact on adjudication.

What does "Case Was Received" mean? +

It means USCIS accepted your filing fee and locked in your priority date. You will next receive biometric or approval notices.

How do I expedite my case with USCIS? +

You can request expedited processing via the USCIS Contact Center if you meet criteria such as severe financial loss or urgent humanitarian emergencies.
