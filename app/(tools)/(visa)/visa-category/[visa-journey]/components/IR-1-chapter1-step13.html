What Happens Next
USCIS approval does not end the immigration journey. The next handoff is usually to the National Visa Center.
Next phase
USCIS forwards the approved petition
After approval, USCIS sends the approved petition to the National Visa Center for immigrant visa pre-processing.
NVC creates the case
Once the petition reaches NVC and the case is entered into its system, the case is prepared for the next visa-processing steps.
You receive the NVC Welcome Letter
The Welcome Letter is the signal that the case is now in the NVC stage and ready for fee, CEAC, and document-preparation work.
Use current NVC timeframes for timing context rather than relying on one fixed wait estimate.
