Why We Built This

The story behind Rahvana

Behind every immigration case is a family trying to become whole again.

Rahvana was built from that reality — not as an abstract idea, but as a response to what we saw people we love go through: complex form codes instead of clear language, critical steps spread across government sites, and long periods of silence while cases moved through opaque systems.

The paperwork is real. So is what it holds — someone waiting at home, children growing up over video calls, milestones spent apart, and the steady effort of holding hope across distance.

That's Rahvana: the space between people who belong together.

In Phase 1, we are focused exclusively on the IR-1 spouse visa journey — to go deep on one path before expanding further.

"We wanted to build something that understood both things: the bureaucratic complexity and the human weight. A tool that holds your hand through the process without losing sight of why you're doing it."
