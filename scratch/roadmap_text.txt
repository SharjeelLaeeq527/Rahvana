Security Roadmap
Secrets & conﬁguration management
SaaS Security
SECRET
VAULT
Never commit .env files, tokens, private keys, or service 
credentials
Use a secret manager; inject secrets at runtime
Rotate keys after exposure, role changes, or vendor changes
Separate dev, staging, and production credentials
Least privilege for cloud IAM and third-party integrations
Pre-commit secret scan + protected CI secrets should be mandatory
